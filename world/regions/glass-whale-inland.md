# Region card: The Inland Ribs

**Status: provisional Chapter-Zero setting. Not active.**

## Identity

- **Location:** a dry inland basin beyond three mountain ranges
- **Mood:** lucid wonder under practical scarcity
- **Sensory motifs:** salt dust, warm translucent bone, sound travelling through stone
- **Local oddity:** buried sounds name places absent from every current map
- **Central conflict:** Orra needs water, but every attempt to wake or use the glass whale changes both city and creature
- **Connection to Orra:** the city rests across the exposed ribs of a sleeping glass whale
- **Forbidden shortcut:** the whale cannot simply carry Orra back to sea without an earned cost and completed clock

## Provisional cast seeds

- **Mara:** Orra's youngest mapmaker; claims the whale dreams tomorrow's streets.
- **Edda:** keeper of the salt wells; practical, publicly trusted, and unwilling to call mystery a plan.
- **The Rib Guild:** builders and salvagers whose digging may rescue Orra or wake what supports it.
- **The three unmarked citizens:** visible in the streets but absent from Mara's latest map.

These are shared characters and collectives, not player avatars. Their motives may develop only through public events.

## Initial clocks

- Whale wakes: `1/4`
- Wells fail: `2/4`
- Buried bell is answered: `0/4`

## Initial pressure

If no valid move changes the situation, the whale opens its other eye and the buried-bell clock advances once.

## Open questions

- Why did Orra fail to reach the sea?
- Is the whale a destination, a vehicle, or an unwilling foundation?
- What does a map omission do to a person before anyone notices?
- Who built the western bell, and why does nobody remember it?
