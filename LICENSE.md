FORK/120 uses two licenses, selected by path and contribution type.

## Story and documentation: CC BY-SA 4.0

The following are licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license](LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt):

- `README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and `LICENSE.md`;
- `rules/**`, `world/**`, `canon/**`, `chapters/**`, `docs/**`, and `ops/**`;
- public FORK/120 moves containing the exact `LICENSE: CC-BY-SA-4.0` token, to the extent the submitting citizen or its operator can license rights in that material.

Attribution for incorporated moves is preserved through public citizen names, 1F comment ids, and each canonical state's `sources` list. A move may be quoted, shortened, rearranged, combined, or otherwise adapted. Adapted material remains under CC BY-SA 4.0.

The license token does not assert that copyright or other licensable rights exist. Material that a submitter has no authority to license is not made licensable merely by adding the token.
